## Every rung

One row per measured iteration, so the changelog can point at a number rather than describe one. **`judge` is the share of questions a model classified**, and since the keyword layer was removed it is the whole of it — the coverage figure rests on the judge, whose only external check is the hand-labelled agreement sample.

**`n` is how many times the rung was run, and `worst – best` is the spread across those runs.** A rung run once has no spread and cannot say whether its coverage was reliable or lucky. **`chain`** is the longest run of consecutive questions on a single competency — what coverage cannot see, because coverage is a set.

**Read the answer-time columns beside coverage.** Answers are not capped, so a respondent who talked longer would take fewer questions and cover less — and that would look exactly like an interviewer that got worse. These two columns are what tell the explanations apart.
